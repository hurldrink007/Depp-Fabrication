About Us

Metalwork done right, on time

Depp Fabrication is a small, family-run metal shop founded by Warren Depp. For over twenty-five years we've built parts and structures for local manufacturers, contractors, and one-off custom jobs — everything from a single bracket to full structural runs.

Our crew does the work start to finish: design help, cutting, machining, welding, and finishing, all under one roof. We keep our team small on purpose. When you call, you talk to the people running the machines.

What We Do

Fabrication & machining services

Custom Metal Fabrication

Brackets, frames, enclosures, and one-off builds cut and assembled to spec.

CNC Machining

Precision milling and turning on our CNC-4500 for tight-tolerance parts.

MIG & TIG Welding

Certified welding on steel, stainless, and aluminum, structural and fine work.

Plasma & Shear Cutting

Clean cuts on plate and bar stock, prototype quantities to production runs.

Structural Steel

Beams, supports, railings, and weldments built to drawing.

Finishing

Grinding, deburring, and powder-coat prep so parts leave ready to install.
